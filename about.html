How I approach building software

I enjoy turning ideas into practical and user-friendly software. My approach starts with understanding the problem, planning the application structure, and then building each part step by step. Whether it is a responsive website, a database-driven application, or a full-stack project, I focus on keeping the interface simple, the code organized, and the application easy to use.

I believe that learning by building is one of the best ways to improve as a developer. Through academic projects, internships, and personal projects, I have worked with frontend technologies, backend development, databases, REST APIs, and different development tools. Each project gives me an opportunity to understand a new technology and improve my problem-solving skills.

As a Computer Engineering student, I am continuously expanding my technical skills. I like exploring new technologies, debugging problems, and improving existing projects rather than simply making something that works once. My goal is to become a strong software engineer who can understand a problem, design a solution, and build it into a reliable application.
